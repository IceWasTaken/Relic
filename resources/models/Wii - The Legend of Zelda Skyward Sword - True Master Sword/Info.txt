True Master Sword extracted from Skyward Sword.

From what I can tell this is the "Master Sword" and "True Master Sword" model. The cross-guard are extended outwards like the "True Master Sword" but default loads the darker "Master Sword" textures. The movment of the cross-guards are controlled by vertex morph that I can't export so the geometry is locked in the shape of the "True Master Sword".

While the "Master Sword" and "True Master Sword" look to be the same model the game uses 2 unique Sheaths for the "Master Sword" and "True Master Sword".

Textures are not broken. The model(s) is made use mirrored texture tiling.

Tools:
Dolphin to extract the .arc.LZ files
Puyo Tools Bin 1.0.7 to decompress .arc.LZ files
BREES Viewer V1.02 to convert to psk files
Blender 2.78 via psk/psa import plug-in to save a .obj
